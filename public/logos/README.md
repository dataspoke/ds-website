**Bottom line:** only **Zapier** and **Mindbody** offer logos that a non-partner can use without asking first. Every other company needs a partner agreement or written permission before you show its logo. Show those companies as plain-text names. ## Rules for the strip as a whole

1. **Do not imply endorsement, partnership or certification.** Every company bans this. Do not use the words "partner", "certified", "authorized" or "official" anywhere near the strip. "Works with the tools you already use" is fine because it is factual and descriptive.
2. **Use logos without changes.** Do not recolor, stretch, crop, add shadows or animate them, and do not merge them into a composite lockup. Showing logos in grey would be a recolor. **Zapier, Mindbody, RingCentral, Google and Meta all forbid altering the marks, so show the downloaded logos in their supplied colors** (Mindbody charcoal, Zapier dark wordmark). A grey CSS filter is a modification. If you want a uniform grey strip, show every company as text.
3. **DataSpoke's own brand must dominate.** Third-party logos must be smaller and less prominent than the DataSpoke logo (Intuit and RingCentral say this outright). Keep them in a separate strip, never in a lockup with the DataSpoke mark.
4. **Use plain-text names for the TEXT ONLY and PERMISSION NEEDED companies.** Use correct spelling and capitals: Salesforce, Lawmatics, Clio, QuickBooks, RingCentral, Google Ads, Meta, Google Workspace, ServiceTitan, NetSuite, PandaDoc. Never abbreviate (no "QB" or "SFDC"). A mixed strip of a few logos plus text names is fine. A text-only strip of the same names in DataSpoke's own font is the safest option.
5. **Add a trademark notice** in the page footer or under the strip, for example: "Salesforce®, QuickBooks®, Google Ads™, Google Workspace™, Meta, Mindbody, Zapier, Clio, Lawmatics, RingCentral, ServiceTitan®, NetSuite and PandaDoc are trademarks of their respective owners. SERVICETITAN is a registered trademark of ServiceTitan, Inc. Google Ads and Google Workspace are trademarks of Google LLC. DataSpoke is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a partner of these companies."
6. **Keep claims accurate.** Only list a tool if Nick has actually built with or connected to it.
7. **Use only official files.** Do not replace these files with copies from Brandfetch, Wikimedia or other third-party sites. If you need a different variant, re-download it from the brand page above.
8. **Respect clear space and minimum size.** Leave padding around each logo of at least half the logo height. Meta's minimum is 12px for the symbol and Salesforce's is 96px wide (partners only). Keep the Zapier and Mindbody wordmarks at 20px tall or more so they stay legible.

## How to unlock more logos

- **Clio:** email api.partnerships@clio.com and mention the Clio API integration.
- **RingCentral:** email the strip mockup to designer@ringcentral.com.
- **ServiceTitan:** email Mcom@servicetitan.com.
- **NetSuite:** email trademar_us@oracle.com.
- **Salesforce, QuickBooks, Google, Meta:** realistically you need to join their partner programs (Salesforce Partner, QuickBooks ProAdvisor or App Partner, Google Partners, Meta Business Partners).
- **Lawmatics:** use the agency partner program at lawmatics.com/partners/agency.
