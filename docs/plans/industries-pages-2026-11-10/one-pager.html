Questions law firms ask

Q. What does an AI and data consultant do for a law firm?

A. An AI and data consultant connects the systems a firm already runs, organizes the information in them and puts it to work. For a law firm, that usually means linking intake, practice management, phones and accounting. Then we build a scorecard for intake and revenue, and set up AI that drafts from the firm's own files. We do not give legal advice, and we do not replace your case management software. Most firms start with intake, because every report and AI tool depends on clean intake records.

Q. Can you connect Clio or Lawmatics to our intake forms, phones and accounting?

A. Yes. We have built on both Lawmatics and Clio for law firms. Common connections send web forms and email into Lawmatics, put RingCentral call logs on the right lead or matter, open a matter in Clio when an intake signs, and carry billing into QuickBooks. We confirm what your setup allows on the free AI strategy call before we promise anything, and if you run different software, we tell you what it would take.

Q. How do we see which marketing source signs cases?

A. We carry the source of each lead from first contact to signed matter and collected fee. Ad spend from Google Ads and Meta Ads, intake records from your CRM and revenue from billing go into one dashboard that updates itself. You see cost per signed case by source and by case type, without anyone exporting a report, so you know which campaigns to grow and which to cut.

Q. How do you keep client data confidential when we use AI?

A. Your data stays in accounts the firm owns, under logins the firm controls. We set up AI tools to read only what you give them access to, and nothing goes to a client or a court until a lawyer approves it. We write down where each kind of data lives and who can see it, so your firm can check it against its own confidentiality duties. Because every account is in the firm's name, the firm can remove our access at any time.

Q. Do we have to replace our case management software?

A. No. We build on the systems you already pay for, such as Clio and Lawmatics. The goal is for those systems to share information, not for your team to learn a new one. Your intake team keeps working in Lawmatics, and your lawyers keep working in Clio.
